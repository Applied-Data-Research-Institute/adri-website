Applied Data Research Institute

At ADRI, we leverage data analytics and advanced systems modeling to address critical challenges facing our ever-evolving world. Guided by science and a commitment to ethics, our mission is to make society more sustainable, equitable, and resilient. We work closely with partners across sectors to identify and forward projects that contribute to these goals.

Who We Are

We are a collection of scientists and researchers who are passionate about finding answers to some of the world's most challenging questions. With expertise in energy, transportation, ecology, land use, and spatial analytics, we work to achieve more sustainable, equitable, and resilient outcomes that support thriving communities now and for generations to come.
